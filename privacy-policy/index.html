Privacy Policy for Linksy

Effective Date: May 09, 2026

Auraluxe ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Linksy mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the Google Play Developer Policy.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide and improve our real-time messaging Service, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you register, we may collect your Email address, Phone number, Name, Username, and Bio.
  • Media & Photos: We collect the images you upload as your Profile Picture. These are securely stored on our cloud servers.
  • Chat Data: As a messaging app, we temporarily and securely store your messages (text, timestamps, sender/receiver IDs) on our servers to deliver them to the intended recipient and sync your chat history across your devices.
  • Usage Data & Device Information: We may automatically collect information such as your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, device type, operating system, and diagnostic data to fix bugs and improve performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Auraluxe uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Linksy App.
  • To manage your account and authenticate your identity.
  • To enable real-time messaging and communication between users.
  • To notify you about changes to our Service or provide customer support.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We use trusted third-party services to power our app infrastructure, which may collect information used to identify you. These services comply with strict security standards:

  • Google Play Services: For app delivery and core Android features.
  • Firebase Authentication: To securely manage user sign-up and login.
  • Cloud Firestore (Firebase): To store user profiles and sync chat messages in real-time.
  • Firebase Storage: To securely host user profile pictures.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Policy (Your Rights)

In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

  • Retention: We retain your personal information and chat history only for as long as your account is active.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account directly within the Linksy app by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account (if available), or by contacting us.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You can request complete data erasure by sending an email to our support team at the email address provided below. Upon request, we will permanently delete your profile, email, profile picture, and all associated messages from our active databases within 30 days.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. Linksy uses industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) provided by Firebase to protect your data in transit and at rest. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us so we can take necessary actions to remove that information from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
