Delete Your Linksy Account

At Linksy, we respect your privacy and give users the ability to request account deletion at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email using your registered Linksy account email address.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Linksy Account
  3. Email us at:

support.linksy@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your deletion request is processed, we may delete:

  1. Your profile information
  2. Your account credentials
  3. Your uploaded profile photos
  4. Your app activity associated with your account

Data Retention

Some data may be temporarily retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance purposes for a limited period.
